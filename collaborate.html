Open Problems & Research Directions

Things I would like to think about.

A brief collection of open questions and directions I am actively exploring. If any of these overlap with your work, feel free to reach out.

Computational Social Choice

Kemeny Rank Aggregation

Can we beat the 4/3 LP barrier with an efficient, practical algorithm?

Known PTAS schemes are too slow for real-world use. I am interested in practical combinatorial algorithms that improve on the 4/3 approximation bound.

Geometry & Metric Embeddings

ε-Coresets in Banach & Transportation Spaces

Does an ε-coreset for a ground metric imply a coreset for the transportation metric over it?

I am looking at lifting clustering coreset constructions beyond Euclidean spaces into general Banach spaces and transportation metrics.

Algorithms & Complexity

Shortest Paths under General Norms

How does norm geometry govern the approximability of shortest paths?

I study approximation barriers and algorithmic schemes for min-norm path problems across polyhedral, smooth, and general norms.

Learning & Control

Algorithmic Foundations of Robotics

How can theoretical guarantees guide sample-efficient robot learning?

An emerging interest focused on bridging reinforcement learning and trajectory planning with worst-case optimization guarantees.
